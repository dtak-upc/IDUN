SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE - FICTIONAL CLINICAL NARRATIVE

Sleep review
Zolpidem was prescribed to treat insomnia. Confusion developed after zolpidem was administered; the clinician attributed the confusion to zolpidem. Zolpidem treatment for insomnia was discontinued because of confusion. Confusion resolved after zolpidem was withdrawn.

Other findings
The sleep review did not discuss a relationship between asthma and zolpidem. No new treatment for asthma was proposed in this review.
