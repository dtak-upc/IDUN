SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE - FICTIONAL CLINICAL NARRATIVE

Respiratory review
Asthma was treated with inhaled albuterol. Wheezing improved after albuterol was administered for asthma. Albuterol treatment for asthma was continued.

Metabolic review
Metformin was prescribed to treat type 2 diabetes. Nausea developed after metformin was started; the clinician attributed the nausea to metformin. Metformin treatment for type 2 diabetes was discontinued because of the nausea. Nausea resolved after metformin was stopped.
